What healthcare roles do you cover?
The current hub scope includes RN and nursing staff, allied-health professionals such as PT, OT, and imaging technicians, healthcare operations leadership, and clinical department heads. A search begins with the actual care setting and work requirement. I do not assume that one department’s staffing need transfers directly to another.
How do you approach a coverage gap?
I begin by clarifying what must be covered, where the role sits in the workflow, and what schedule or operational conditions shape success. That makes the outreach brief more useful and helps separate candidates whose background is broadly similar from candidates who can operate in the stated environment.
Can you support confidential healthcare leadership searches?
Confidentiality is especially important when a leadership search affects a department, care team, or ongoing operational transition. I use direct, discreet outreach rather than a public-first process, then share the mandate only as appropriate in the candidate conversation. The goal is to protect the organization while testing genuine fit.
